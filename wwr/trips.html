Widowmaker Falls

Our most intense run, built for experienced paddlers who want maximum white water. Sharp drops and fast technical sections keep every second demanding. Full safety briefing and gear included

Half Day Adventure

A shorter route that still delivers real rapids without an all-day commitment. Great for visitors on a tight schedule or first-time rafters easing into the sport.

Sunrise Expedition

A calmer, scenic float departing at dawn, ideal for families, photographers, and anyone who wants river views without heavy rapids.
